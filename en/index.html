Mishkah

A bookshop that reads like a book

«متجر يُقرأ كما يُقرأ الكتاب»

Most themes are built to show photographs; Mishkah is built to show words. An Arabic-first theme for bookshops, publishers, stationery, calligraphy supplies and educational and digital products. This guide covers every section and every setting, so you can build your store with confidence.

Quick start

Try Mishkah free, open the editor, make five settings first, then arrange your sections.

  1. 1

    Try it free

    Open Mishkah’s page in the Salla theme store and try it on your store before you buy; Salla’s theme store lets you try a paid theme free before buying.

  2. 2

    Install it

    After the trial, install Mishkah from its page in the theme store. It is ready on install: the homepage arrives with its default sections, with no setup.

  3. 3

    Open the editor

    Press Customize (تخصيص) on the theme in your Salla dashboard to open the theme editor, where you add and arrange homepage sections and adjust the theme’s settings.

Five settings to start with

  1. 1Logo or store name

    In the Header group, turn on Store name in the display font عرض اسم المتجر بخط العناوين بدل الشعار to set your store name in Amiri instead of a logo, or Logo and store name together الشعار واسم المتجر معاً في رأس الصفحة to show both. For a storefront-only logo use Storefront logo شعار الواجهة, and for the browser tab Browser icon أيقونة المتصفح.

  2. 2Store colour

    The colour you choose for your store in Salla drives the whole design. If it is very light (yellow or sky blue), set Primary colour strength قوة اللون الأساسي to strong in the Identity & type group, and pick the Second metal المعدن الثاني (الزخارف والتوهج).

  3. 3Header

    Choose the Header style نمط الرأس, and replace the Announcement text نص الإعلان with your store’s real policy; the default line is a sample about free shipping. The Hijri date and the Night reading button stay in the header unless you turn them off.

  4. 4Footer

    Your VAT number, commercial registration, freelance certificate and Saudi Business Center verification are read from your store’s details in Salla; if they are not there, enter them in the Footer (the colophon) group. Add a Short about text نبذة قصيرة (تظهر بدل وصف المتجر), and choose whether the social icons use their platforms’ colours.

  5. 5Payment logos

    The payment methods active on your store show by themselves. In the Payment logos group, switch on the official logos of anything else you accept, from 19: mada and Apple Pay through to Tabby and Tamara.

Add and arrange sections

In the editor, open the homepage and add a section from Mishkah’s 51, then drag sections into the order you want; each section has its own settings panel. This guide sorts them into nine groups to help you choose.

Some sections stay hidden until you add their content: Reels Rail needs at least one valid clip, and Category Rail needs each disc linked to a real category from your store.

Heroes & video

8 sections

The first thing a visitor sees: a hero led by video, type, covers or search, and clips cropped into the lamp-niche arch or set beside your books. Clips load only near the viewport and respect Save-Data and reduced motion.

Video Hero

واجهة الفيديو

Default on install

A full-width hero: a still image paints first, then the video loads once the page has finished and starts muted, under a large headline with two buttons. Phones fall back to the still under Save-Data, a 2G connection or reduced motion.

Useful settings

Video link (mp4 or webm) رابط الفيديو (mp4 أو webm)
a direct link to a clip on your own store storage (the editor’s media gallery takes video); 1080p suits desktop.
Phone video link (optional) رابط فيديو الجوال (اختياري)
a lighter cut of the same clip (e.g. 720p) loaded on phones; empty uses the same video everywhere.
Still image (shown first) الصورة الثابتة (تُعرض أولاً)
1920×1080; it stays visible behind the video.
Hero height ارتفاع الواجهة
half, three-quarters or full screen; half is recommended, keeping the first shelf in view.
Overlay on the video طبقة التعتيم فوق الفيديو
ink, brass or none; the scrim is what keeps the text readable.
The clip has sound المقطع يحتوي على صوت
shows a mute/unmute button; the video always starts muted and sound plays only on the visitor’s tap, so keep any sound free of music.

البطل الطباعي

Background colour لون الخلفية
paper, primary or ink.
Show the Hijri date عرض التاريخ الهجري
worked out with the Umm al-Qura calendar; it may differ by moon sighting.

Video Niche

مشهد المشكاة

Useful settings

Crop shape شكل القصّ
an arch that opens with the scroll, a wide band with a moving zoom, or an arch beside the text.
Video link (mp4 or webm) رابط الفيديو (mp4 أو webm)
a direct link on your own storage; the theme editor’s media gallery accepts video.
Still image (optional) صورة ثابتة (اختيارية)
shows until the clip loads, and in its place under Save-Data.

Reels Rail

مقاطع المكتبة

A horizontally scrolled rail of vertical 9:16 clips: direct .mp4 files loop silently as living covers, while YouTube and Vimeo clips play only on a click. Each clip can be linked to a title whose name and price sit under the card.

Useful settings

Clip link (YouTube / Vimeo / mp4 file) رابط المقطع (YouTube / Vimeo / ملف mp4)
a full address starting with https://; anything else drops the clip from the rail.
Cover image (portrait) صورة الغلاف (رأسية)
portrait, 720×1280.
Linked title الكتاب المرتبط بالمقطع
its name and price appear under the card as a plain link.
Card width عرض البطاقة
narrow, standard or wide.
Note
the section does not render at all until at least one valid clip is added: a full URL plus a portrait poster.

Video Shelf

فيديو بجانب الرف

Useful settings

4 to 12 (8 by default).

at the start of the row (the right edge in Arabic) or at its end.

niche arch or a soft-cornered rectangle.

Useful settings

Publisher from the brand

Questions about a book on WhatsApp

Images & video

Mishkah’s guidance for images and clips: modest, free of music, and sized for each section.

Modest imagery

Every image and video field in Mishkah carries the same guidance, in Arabic and English:

  • No woman’s face appears, ever.
  • Any human imagery is back-view, hands-only, flat-lay or ghost-mannequin, fully covered.
  • Safest of all is an image or clip with no people: books and manuscripts, paper and ink, library shelves and calligraphy tools.
  • Mishkah’s demo images and clips show no women’s faces and carry no music; customers’ profile photos are hidden beside reviews; and the halal badge shows only your certificate’s own wording.

Clips

  • No music: a silent clip, or sound effects only.
  • Upload the clip to the media gallery in the theme editor, which takes MP4, MOV and WEBM up to 250 MB. Then paste its direct link into the video field; the fields ask for an mp4 or webm link. Video is never bundled with the theme.
  • For the Video Hero: a 1080p clip for desktop, and a lighter cut (e.g. 720p) in the phone video field.
  • Clips load only as they near the screen and pause when they leave it; they respect Save-Data and reduced motion, showing the still image instead.
  • In the Reels Rail and the Video Gallery, YouTube and Vimeo links are accepted and play only on a click. In the Reels Rail, a link always starts with https://.
  • Safe ideas to film: hands turning pages, ink on paper, a slow pan along the shelves, or a flat-lay of tools.

FAQ

Questions merchants often ask, answered from the theme’s own settings.

My products have no photos. What will customers see?

Any product without a photo appears as a cloth-bound book with a gilt title, so a customer never sees an empty box; that is Automatic typographic cover for products without photos غلاف طباعي تلقائي للمنتجات بلا صور, on by default. To make every shelf uniform, turn on Typographic cover for every product on cards غلاف طباعي لكل المنتجات في البطاقات, and for the product page and cart Typographic cover on the product page and cart too الغلاف الطباعي في صفحة المنتج والسلة أيضاً. Typographic cover style شكل الغلاف الطباعي chooses between the bound book and the quiet paper cover.

How do I show my VAT number in the footer?

If you add the number in Salla’s tax settings, it shows automatically with its certificate and the official VAT mark. Otherwise, enter it in VAT number الرقم الضريبي in the Footer (the colophon) group. The same goes for Commercial registration number رقم السجل التجاري, Freelance certificate number رقم وثيقة العمل الحر and Saudi Business Center verification link رابط التوثيق في المركز السعودي للأعمال.

Can I hide WhatsApp from the social icons?

Yes: turn off WhatsApp beside the social icons واتساب بجانب أيقونات التواصل in the Footer (the colophon) group. To show the icons in your store colour rather than their platforms’ colours, turn off Social icons in their platforms’ colours أيقونات التواصل بألوان منصاتها.

How do I change the text of the “Offers” tab?

In the The “Offers” side tab group, type it in Tab text نص الشريط; left empty, it shows “Offers” in the store language. The same group changes the link (/offers by default) and the side, or turns the tab off. On the homepage the tab opens the Welcome Offer window when you have added it.

Is the theme mobile friendly?

Yes. It is built for the phone first and responsive on every screen: a fixed bottom navigation bar, a sticky buy bar on product pages, two-column shelves by default (1 to 3 with Products per row, mobile عدد المنتجات في الصف — جوال (Products per row, mobile)), a lighter phone video in the Video Hero, and clips that respect Save-Data.

Does it support English?

Yes: Arabic and English with complete translation files. Section text fields are multilingual, and elements placed by start and end, such as the floating dock and the WhatsApp button, mirror automatically in English.

How do I make night mode the default, or hide its button?

In the Night reading group, set Default mode الوضع الافتراضي to day, night or the device’s setting, and turn off Enable night mode تفعيل الوضع الليلي (زر في رأس الصفحة) to remove the button from the header.

Where does the Hijri date appear?

In the header’s top bar (on by default), in the Hijri & Notice Bar section and beside the Gregorian year in the footer. It can also show in the Typographic Hero, Events & Signings and Blog Column sections and on countdown end dates. It is worked out in the browser with the Umm al-Qura calendar and may differ by moon sighting.

Which payment logos can I show?

The payment methods active on your store appear automatically. In the Payment logos group there are 19 switches for the official logos of anything else you accept: mada, Visa and Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Samsung Pay, STC Bank, SADAD, Tabby, Tamara, Emkan, MISpay, Madfu, PayPal, bank transfer, cash on delivery, KNET, BenefitPay, QPay and OmanNet.

How do I add a video to the homepage?

Upload the clip as MP4 or WEBM to the media gallery in the editor (the gallery takes MP4, MOV and WEBM up to 250 MB), then paste its direct link into a video section: the Video Hero, the Video Niche, the Video Shelf or a tile in the Bento Mosaic. The Reels Rail and the Video Gallery also accept YouTube and Vimeo links. Keep the clip silent or to sound effects only, with no music.

Why is the WhatsApp button not showing?

The button needs a WhatsApp number: enter it in WhatsApp number رقم واتساب in the WhatsApp on every page group, or add it to your store’s details. The Floating Dock belongs to the homepage; for a button on every page, turn on Floating WhatsApp button on every page زر واتساب عائم في كل صفحات المتجر.

The zakat calculator shows no result. Why?

Enter today’s gold price per gram in Gold price per gram سعر جرام الذهب, in your store currency, and keep it current; without it the calculator works out neither the nisab nor the zakat due, because a stale price could wrongly tell a customer that zakat is not due. The calculator is a guide only and never collects money.
